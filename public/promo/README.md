# Promo banner photo

Save the lifestyle photo for the lime "Bedsitter Glow-Up" banner here as **glow-up.jpg**.

- Landscape or square, about 1200 × 900 px, under ~300 KB (compress at squoosh.app or tinypng.com).
- The photo fills the whole banner, with the text over a dark fade on the left — keep the main subject in the centre or right. Wide (landscape) photos work best.
- Then commit and push; Vercel publishes it automatically.

Different file name? Update `promoPhoto` in `src/config.ts`.
